Plan facilities

Where each activity runs. Defaults come from the Structures registry (one per activity); overrides are plan-scoped — the registry stays untouched. Every calculation multiplies against these: material requirements (ME rig), job durations (TE rig + structure hull), and install cost (system cost index × facility tax).
