# Ragnarok mockup set

The titan set — same pages, layout, and components as the
reference standard ([`../typhoon/`](../typhoon/README.md), see
[`../README.md`](../README.md) for the binding convention),
populated with the Ragnarok ×1 chain: the **absolute chain stress
test**, the program's final chain set. The Hel's surfaces at
double the duration and scale — the reaction **year** (350 days at
~10 reactors against the Hel's 131), the 4-level skill gap (a
~4-month train against the Hel's 3-level 2-month), the
billion-Pyerite mountain (7.6× the Hel's), and four titan-specific
**mineral-sink** components that add procurement volume rather
than reaction depth.

| File | Shows |
|---|---|
| `planner.html` | Commodity-first search and target, Plan participants (Octavia's 4-level underleveled gate — the state at maximum; reaction slots as the binding constraint at 2.7× the Hel's), 82-type blueprint tier (44 owned byte-for-byte the Hel's / 38 missing incl. the four titan BPs), six-phase intermediates summary at record scale, raw summaries grouped around the billion-mountain, reaction-throughput card (the year arithmetic — the Hel's card at 2.7×) |
| `build-plan.html` | The terminal wave bar (420 d across seven segments; the reaction year owns 45%, the hull 16%), six-phase chain flow with the titan mineral-sink flavor at phase 5, representative phase-tree nodes (AIPS 1,067 runs · the four titan parts), the Hel's absent-copy state carried over, 82-blueprint inventory, billion-scale acquisition meters (Pyerite 0.06%), wave-1 allocation with Octavia's parallel 4-month train |
| `build-plan-active.html` | Day 210 of 420 (50%): the reaction year 70% through its window, the carbon family complete and feeding the tiers, the intermediates ledger as a logistics instrument (206,200 Carbon Fiber held across two consuming levels), the double-gated target (components + the final train level, 119 days of slack), the early-BPC carrying-cost observation at its extreme (eleven months idle), and **mineral tranches** — the mountain's staged purchases against the year's gradual consumption, a procurement-scheduling surface no prior set needed |

## Data provenance

- Quantities, phases, runs, and the throughput arithmetic come
  from `reference/chains/RAGNAROK-CHAIN.md` (the model runs at ×1
  against the live SDE mirror, release 2026-11-20). Component
  bills (AIPS 1,067 runs · the four titan parts at maxPL 40),
  reaction counts (27,979), durations (70.8 d skilled hull), and
  the 82/82 type counts are SDE data; the Ragnarok BPC's contract
  acquisition and the supercapital facility class are the Hel's
  pins carried over.
- Blueprint ownership from the live `blueprints` table, pulled
  2026-11-21: 82 types — 44 owned (identical to the Hel's: every
  prior chain's world intact) and 38 missing. Stock figures real:
  Pyerite 652,411 against 1.117B (0.06%), Tritanium 97.4M against
  318.8M (31% — the largest single coverage in the program), all
  19 gases at zero.
- **Skills simulated only for Capital Ship Construction 5** — the
  underleveled-holder state at maximum (Octavia at 1, the sole
  holder, training from day 0; the final level green two waves
  before install). Every reaction gate held live at 5.
- Wave decomposition (the 420-day chain end, wave boundaries, the
  train's completion timing), intermediates movements, tranche
  schedules, and market figures are **invented for the wireframe**
  — illustrative at maximum looseness (the merged-campaign and
  pipelining decisions move quarters; the honest rendering is a
  plan whose precision is seasonal, not daily).
