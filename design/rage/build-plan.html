Four one-run copies = four parallel invention slots for the top-up's four attempts — the same sizing rule the Trimark set carries (BPC count = min(attempt budget, slot pool); copy time split-invariant), defused by this chain's constants: 8 min/run against the Trimark's 60, 4 attempts against 30. Invention plan — the top-up

One Rage success tops up the 2-run shortfall; the same attempts roll the Javelin sibling at its own 34%. Successes counted from synced industry jobs; a shortfall re-arms per the retry semantics — at this scale the loop is one batch and, at worst, one re-arm.

Source Attempts Consumes Decryptor Window Confidence
Scourge HAM BPC ×4 4 4 BPC runs · 4 + 4 datacores (stock covers exactly) none — p 0.34 · BPC at 10 runs / ME 2 / TE 4 (the live-evidence base) 12.67 h P(≥1 Rage) 81% · E 1.4 successes

Expected outcome: one 10-run Rage BPC against the 2-run shortfall — 8 spare runs of BPC residue banked on the pool, never inflating the target. Each attempt also rolls the Javelin sibling: E ≈ 1.4 sibling successes at n = 4, so roughly one 10-run Javelin BPC (≈50,000 missiles) of by-product joins the 8,595 Javelins already held. No decryptor at these odds — the live line's Attainment choice (p_eff 61%) was for bulk production; a 4-attempt top-up doesn't justify the ME/TE cost, and the owned BPCs' stats show what Attainment produced when it mattered.

wireframe This chain owns the over-delivery half of the residue story (the Trimark set owns under-delivery and the retry loop): surplus BPCs and sibling by-products render as banked inventory on the active plan. The sibling semantics — by-product or miss, and whether both land on one attempt — stay an M8 pin; the 8,595 Javelins in inventory are the live precedent that by-products happen on this very line.
