Residue is tracked, not hidden: banked BPCs and spare runs are plan-visible inventory with a future-plan disposition; reaction surpluses are the batch-minimum floor, known at plan time. Nothing here inflates the 500,000 target — surplus is an asset, not a debt to consume.
wireframe The residue card is this set's contribution: the over-delivery half of the probabilistic-outcome story, shown as first-class inventory rather than buried in prose. Its asymmetry is deliberate — surplus BPCs bank forward, they never grow the target; the Trimark set carries the mirror card (material held while short). The Javelin sibling landing mid-batch is the live answer to the alternative- product question this chain surfaced: by-products happen, and the planner should show them, not fold them into "successes".
