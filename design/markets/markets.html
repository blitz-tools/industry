Markets

The synced market list and its world-state band — the planner's sell-side market selector reads the enabled set, the same way the facilities section reads the Structures registry. Division of record: industry facilities in the structure manager, markets here.

wireframe The second of the three surface mockups (M15 sync panel, after M3's structures page — same registry/status shape against the reference component set). The market list is user state feeding planner selectors; the sync families and rate-limit headroom below are its observability subset — Admin > Observability holds the central view (nav entry added above), and nothing here duplicates those checks: one stored dataset, page-filtered.
