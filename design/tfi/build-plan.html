wireframe The faction-tier bar: the acquisition front owns 83% of the plan — not minerals, not reactions, but a currency earned through gameplay. The build world (W1–W2) is 2.5 days of fully owned, fully green operations. This is the inverse of every prior set: the Hel's front was shopping (dead 9%), the Crash chain's body was reactions (52%) — here the plan is mostly waiting on LP, and the planner's honest rendering is a long dead zone with a slim, certain build behind it. If the curated corp balance covers the purchase, the front collapses to the distribution step (~a day) and the plan is ~3.5 d end-to-end — the what-if the pool picker answers.

Blueprint copy plan

Nothing to copy — the fourth distinct absent-copy cause: the target's BPCs arrive as purchased copies (the LP store's product), and the component world runs from owned originals with ample maxPL.

wireframe The absent-copy states now number four: "no copy activity exists" (Crash), "nothing to copy from" (Jackdaw), "the BPC is the acquired item" (Hel — contract), and now "the BPCs are the purchase" (TFI — LP store). Same rendered state, four remediation stories — the modeling set grows one per tier, and the pattern suggests the app should carry an explicit acquisition-path label on every blueprint rather than inferring it.
