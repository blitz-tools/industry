Blueprint copy plan — invention inputs

Copies feeding invention, not manufacturing: BPC count is the invention-parallelism knob (one attempt per BPC, job-lock), and total copy time is split-invariant — 2 × 18-run copies and 30 × 1-run copies cost the same BPO-hours, so the split is a pure parallelism decision.

wireframe First chain whose copy plan is Copy → Invention (mandatory — invention consumes BPC runs, BPOs can't be invention sources), the destination the copy-destinations decision records. The sizing rule: BPC count = min(attempt budget, invention slot pool); runs per BPC = ⌈budget ÷ count⌉. The two corp BPOs are the wall-clock bottleneck asset — a third would cut the 15 h copy floor to 10 h.

Invention plan

Ten successes at 34% as a replenishment loop, not a one-shot phase: batch 1 installs with the copies, successes are counted from synced industry jobs when it completes, and a shortfall re-arms the next batch sized to the remaining need. The target never silently shrinks — 7 successes is a blocked plan with an owner decision, not a 7-rig plan.

batch 2 — conditional 8 8 BPC runs · 24 + 24 datacores · 8 copy jobs added (4 h) none — p 0.34 (escalation option at the decision point) 6.5 h re-armed on shortfall: P(≥ remaining) 82%

Batch 1 is sized to expectation (E[successes] 10.2 = the ten needed) rather than to confidence — the 40% shortfall branch costs one re-arm wave (+10.5 h including the added copies), while sizing batch 1 to 80% up front would spend 105 + 105 datacores before any information arrives. Expected residue at the 30-attempt batch: E[successes] 10.2 against 10 — a razor-thin ≈0.2 surplus BPCs; the 80% budget would expect ≈2. Failed attempts consume their BPC run either way — the attempt budget is the spend, success or fail.

wireframe The retry-semantics direction realized: attempts in batches, a memoryless re-arm sized to the remaining need, and a budget ceiling with an owner decision at exhaustion — extend budget, escalate decryptor (Attainment p_eff 61% at ME/TE cost), buy BPCs, or descope. Never auto-escalates spend. Residue is tracked in both directions: surplus BPCs bank for future plans (never inflating this target); provisioned-but-unconsumed salvage stays visible against the plan while blocked. The invention→manufacturing edge is pipelining-eligible (each rig job needs only its own BPC + salvage) — v1 keeps the phases serial.
