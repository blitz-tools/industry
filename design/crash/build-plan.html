Blueprint copy plan

Nothing to copy — no copy plan exists for this chain, and none could: reaction formulas carry no copy activity at all (activity 11 only), and the booster blueprint needs a single max-run job, so copying it buys nothing.

Parallelism in this chain comes from buying more cheap originals — one formula per reactor per line (≈4 each), the same economics as the T2-component BPOs in the Panther chain. The absent-copy state differs from the Jackdaw's: there, relics weren't copyable and drops supplied BPCs; here, originals throughout — the entire chain runs without a single blueprint copy.

wireframe The second absent-copy case, with a different cause — worth rendering distinctly if the app ever collapses them: "no copy activity exists" vs "no blueprint to copy from" vs "copies exist but stock covers". The M9 surface edge cases accumulate one per chain.
