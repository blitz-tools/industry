wireframe T2 components build at 1 unit per run with huge maxProductionLimits — the armor plate's 18,750 runs fit a single 130-hour max-run job, so job counts stay low while ME (Material Efficiency) on the component BPO is the whole cost story (each ME point ≈ 1% of the bill). The missing-blueprint treatment differs from the Charon's: T2 component BPOs are NPC-seeded originals (no producer in the SDE — no contract backfill, no invention), so their badge reads "NPC market" and acquisition is a market buy.

Invention plan

The invented blueprint is a parallel branch — it gates only the final build, so the batch installs in wave 1. One attempt per source BPC (job-lock); attempts run concurrently in science slots. Expected value 2.2 successes per 10-attempt batch. blitzrage is out-of-corp: the source BPCs, 640 datacores, and decryptors are contracted to blitzrage before wave 1 installs — the distribution step is a plan item with its own procurement lead (see planner-scoped.html for the pool without blitzrage).

wireframe The decryptor choice is a calculated-plan decision (the M8 question: fixed per plan vs comparing EVs in the UI — this plan demonstrates the fixed choice). Process is selected for the material axis: ME_eff −1 vs −4 without a decryptor saves ≈3% of the entire ship bill — at 18,750 armor plates and 5.4M Tritanium, that dwarfs the ≈93 h and ≈55 datacores of each type that Optimized Attainment (p_eff 42%, ME_eff −3) would save. A fixed choice also keeps material quantities deterministic while invention is in flight. Failed attempts simply don't produce a BPC — the batch's 92% confidence is the gate on the final phase, presented as expected + confidence, never a deterministic promise. Retries (if all ten fail, 8% odds) append another 53.33 h window with copied or purchased source BPCs.
