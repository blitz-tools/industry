Recipe (identical across grades): 3 + 3 datacores · 1 relic per attempt · 1 h per attempt · skills 1/1/1. One Intact success over-delivers — 20 runs against a ×1 target; the surplus is inventory, not waste. The grade dominates every deterministic axis (relics, datacores, and wall time per ship all fall as the grade rises — Wrecked needs 12.5× Intact's relics per ship), so the choice is price-only — use the highest grade the market stocks (the M10 question). Recipe shared across all four racial tactical destroyers; the job targets the Jackdaw.
wireframe All numbers real: probabilities, runs, recipe, and durations from the SDE mirror; relic and datacore stock from the live tables (2026-11-19 — relics 0, datacores 12 + 97). The batch decision on the calculated plan is attempts-to-buy (confidence), not successes-needed — one success satisfies any target up to runs-per-success. Invention is a parallel branch with no job-lock: each attempt consumes its own relic, so attempts parallelize across science slots freely.
