Who the plan may assign work to — set before Calculate, because eligibility depends on it: skill gates, wave allocation, and the stock meters all resolve against this pool. 3 · Reaction throughput — the bottleneck analysis

The owned-copies overlay slot becomes the throughput table: this chain's defining arithmetic is reaction slot-hours, not blueprint counts or build time — the wall-clock is set by how many parallel reactors the pool provides.

Total: 10,478 runs · 31,434 slot-hours — at the pool's ~10 parallel reactors that is 131 days of reactions alone, dwarfing the 47-day hull build. Formulas are cheap originals (parallelism = one per reactor); slots are not. The manufacturing layer (4,201 runs, mostly minutes-per-run components) schedules around the reaction tiers.

The merged-campaign note: with every fuel type recurring at three levels and six reaction products at two, per-phase campaigns would duplicate thousands of runs — merging is definitional here (M5/M9).
