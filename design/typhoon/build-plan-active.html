wireframe The running estimate moves with two clocks: input cost only as purchases actually clear (committed ≠ spent), sell price at wave boundaries when the synced 30-day average refreshes — small drifts show as the delta against the calculated figure, and a negative-profit plan renders the tile pink with the same shape. Nothing has sold at day 1.6 (ships deliver day 9.5); the realized column starts at first sale. Figures illustrative until M15's price sync. The verdict table lists open decisions only: a build-verdict row retires when its jobs install, a buy-verdict row when its purchase clears — settled rows roll into the realized ledger above instead of disappearing. A fresh plan lists everything (all verdicts open); a fully-committed plan mid-flight lists nothing (tiles + ledger only). Plan activation itself — the first job install, the Draft → Active transition — changes where the numbers come from (estimates gain actuals), never what's listed; the per-commodity commitment clock does that.
